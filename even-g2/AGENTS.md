# Pome Even G2 releases

Josh has authorized every Pome G2 update to be published to the existing Even Hub
Beta channel after appropriate tests and packaging. Do not stop at a Private
upload or ask for repeat permission to promote to Beta. Preserve the existing
testing group. Public store release and review submission are separate actions.

In Even Hub, open the uploaded build, click its Private status badge, select Beta,
then Promote to Beta. Verify the Builds page identifies the new version as the
published Beta build before reporting release success. Distinguish published,
installed, and glasses-tested status.
