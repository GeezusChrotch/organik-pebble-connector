Organik Apps Connector 1.0.0

- Consistent sidebar navigation for Pebble and Even G2, separate device buttons, and a fixed title area when scrolling.
- Shared Tailscale status, clearer setup steps, and repairs beside the relevant errors.
- Calendar permission recovery and improved Beepster contact-name matching.
- Updated bundled DayFrame 0.1.10 and Beepster 0.1.21 web apps for Even G2.
- Background status refreshes keep unrelated controls available.

This GitHub edition supports Notesy, Beepster, Reminderz and Eventz for Pebble, plus DayFrame and Beepster for Even G2. Pome is coming soon to the App Store and is unavailable in this download. The App Store edition retains Pome.

Keep the Mac awake and online, with Tailscale connected on the Mac and paired phone. When switching editions, quit Connector, replace the app without deleting its data, and follow the transition checklist to review permissions and test each paired app.
