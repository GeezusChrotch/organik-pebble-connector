# Organik Apps Connector 1.0.0 — direct-download candidate

- Supports Notesy, Beepster, Reminderz and Eventz on Pebble, plus Beepster and DayFrame on Even G2.
- Shared Tailscale status with separate app connection checks.
- Pome is marked “Coming to the Apple App Store soon”; Apple Home controls and cameras are unavailable in this direct edition.
- Requires an awake, online Mac and Tailscale on both Mac and paired phone.
- Includes File → Open Connector and optional menu-bar operation.

Candidate status: not published. Packaging validation does not establish clean installation or hardware acceptance. Beepster dictation failure is under investigation; Notesy 1.4.8 is bundled with the verified PDF helper launch fix.
